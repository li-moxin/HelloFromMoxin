My name is Moxin Li (李墨馨). I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the College of Computing & Artificial Intelligence, University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Sharon Li. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National University of Singapore (NUS) in 2026, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Mong Li Lee and Prof. Wynne Hsu. I received my Ph.D. from NUS, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Tat-Seng Chua and mentored by Prof. Fuli Feng and Prof. Wenjie Wang. I received my bachelor's degree from Yuanpei College, Peking University, in 2020.

Research Interests

My research focuses on Trustworthy and Safe Agents. My current interests include:

  • Long-Horizon Attacks: Evaluating agent vulnerabilities to multi-turn and cross-interaction attacks. [TROJail] [Plant, Persist, Trigger]
  • Runtime Defense and Agent Harnesses: Developing runtime defenses and adaptive harnesses to improve agent safety while preserving task utility. Preprints coming soon.
  • Additionally, I am interested in agent skills and multi-agent collaboration.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration. Feel free to reach out!
